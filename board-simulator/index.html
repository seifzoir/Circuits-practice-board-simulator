Board Simulator Controls & Shortcuts

  • Mains: switch the board on with the green rocker switch on the board itself.
  • Wiring: drag from any socket (or multimeter jack) to another, or click one then the other. Several plugs can share a socket.
  • Multimeters: click + Add Multimeter (or press D). Their four jacks start empty — connect them with patch cords like any other socket.
  • Components: drag them from the sidebar onto sockets. Double-click a resistor / capacitor / inductor to change its value (Ω, kΩ, MΩ …). Turn the variable resistor's knob with the mouse.
  • Phone / tablet: pinch with two fingers to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan; one finger on an empty spot also pans. Touch near a socket and drag to draw a wire (or tap one socket, then another). Tap a part or a cable to select it — the buttons next to a part rotate, edit or delete it, and the Delete button in the toolbar removes the selection. Open the parts drawer with Components.
  • Select & Delete: click a wire or a component to select it, then press Delete / Backspace.
